Backtest Ollama Casual is a research rig that answers the question nobody in the paid-signals industry wants asked: **where does the edge actually live?** An Ollama LLM (`gemma4:31b-cloud` via [json-inference](https://github.com/tripolskypetr/json-inference)) parses each Telegram signal-seller's own guidance—text and chart screenshots—the engine executes it within minutes of publication, and event studies over minute candles reveal whether the signal, the money management, or neither carries the alpha.

## What This Repo Measures

The two shipped channels illustrate opposite signal formats and produced answers that were more interesting than "scam / not scam":

<Steps>
  <Step title="The direction has a real micro-edge—but not where the seller says">
    24 hours after a post, price moved the signalled way in **13/18 cases (72%)**, mean **+1.42%** unleveraged. In the first 15–60 minutes: nothing (slightly negative). The edge lives at the 12–24h horizon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The seller's own plan is mathematically ruinous">
    Across 16 entries, price reached TP1 in 44%, TP2 in 19%, TP3 in 6%, TP4/TP5: never. Following posts as written has an EV around **−3.5…−4% per trade unleveraged**—and advertised X13–25 leverage turns a single stop into −60…−100% of margin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="All realized profit came from exit engineering">
    Level-ratchet trailing alone: **−0.31%** on the month. Adding a structural breakeven rule: **+3.37%**. The ratchet slack (0.3 of the grid step) is derived from the process distribution, not fitted to PnL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every claim is reproducible">
    All numbers come from `measure-items` + `candle-items` in MongoDB, with the scripts shipped in `content/*/assets/`. One month, 16–18 events per claim—hypothesis-grade, and the repo says so out loud.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Design Principles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="LLM as Parser, Not Oracle" icon="brain">
    The model extracts structured data (prices, direction, targets) from natural language posts. Tool-call grammar via json-inference forces a valid object on every response—`JSON.parse` never fails.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zero Look-Ahead Bias" icon="clock">
    `scrapeLookback` with `when` from the backtest tick ensures the strategy only sees posts that existed at that moment. No future data leaks into the backtest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structural Rules Beat Magic Numbers" icon="ruler">
    Thresholds come from process distributions (retrace percentiles, grid geometry), not from PnL fitting. Constants must survive walk-forward before being trusted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prompt Debugging is a Regression Suite" icon="bug">
    `validate.mjs` runs mandatory cases through the exact pipeline on the live model. Exit code 1 on any failure prevents silent prompt regressions after edits.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
